How to read it
  • Top row: current glucose, trend arrow and change since the last reading.
  • Dots: one column per 5 minutes, newest on the right. Green in range, yellow near a limit, red out of range.
  • Top-left column: minutes since the last reading, one pink pixel per minute.
  • Orange bars: carbs. Teal bars: bolus. Dim blue area: insulin on board.
  • Purple marks on the limit lines: exercise.
  • Red mark top-left: data is stale.
